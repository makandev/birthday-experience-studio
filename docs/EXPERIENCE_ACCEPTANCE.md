## Weaknesses found and repaired

- Mobile preview was too far below the creator controls: moved actual preview first and deferred the traditional navigation.
- Long recipient CTA crowded the back control: bounded flexible width/wrapping.
- Opening navigation could extend below the preview viewport: compact header/opening spacing; viewport control geometry regression.
- Initial placeholder srcdoc caused blank paint in Chromium: removed the second initial document rather than weakening sandbox/CSP.
- Parent compatibility click could close the newly opened letter: narrow startup suppression with explicit deliberate pointer/keyboard regression.
- Newly selected lazy image was asserted before decode: wait for actual loaded dimensions; do not relax integrity assertion.
- Automatic preview scrolling could move a control during a tap: suppress late scroll after intervening creator interaction.
- Finale reuses a public sentence intentionally: quote treatment distinguishes that echo from the late letter and keeps authored content escaped.
